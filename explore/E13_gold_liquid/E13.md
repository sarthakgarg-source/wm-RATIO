on one data set, from one date, twice: as before, and with the switch. The difference is the switch's effect.

## The switch

- **Gold on SELL** means the desk's Weekly LW supertrend on MCX gold is SELL at the decision week. This is the signal E10
  and E11 use: E10's functions, gold's own weekly bars with real highs and lows, a bar usable once its week has ended on
  gold's calendar.
- **With the switch:** at every decision week, the weight a strategy gives gold moves to the liquid ETF while gold is on
  SELL. The other weights are unchanged. In E10's S1 and S4, cash (which earned nothing) is held in the liquid ETF.
- **As before:**
  - Rule R, momentum and the book are exactly their old rules;
  - E10's S1 and S4 hold cash at zero return, as in E10;
  - a strategy can hold gold while it is SELL (Rule R and momentum ignore the supertrend; E11's neutral, ST-TL, MO and
    REF can).
- **The liquid ETF** is a cash level with one price a day. It is read as of each day (the latest level on or before it)
  and bought and sold at that price, at the same 10.4 bps per side as everything else. The count of days that use an
  earlier day's level is reported.

## Dates

- **First decision:** the first decision week of 2012. Rule R's 52-week band and the 12-month momentum have their year

## The families (all as in their studies; weekly decisions, next-session fills, 10.4 bps per side)

- **R — Rule R on N100 ÷ gold, MC100 ÷ gold, SC100 ÷ gold** (T03's rule, the core of E03–E07 and E09):
  - first week below mean − 1 sd → hold the index;
  - above mean + 1 sd → hold gold;
  - back at the mean → 50/50.

  The pair frame, the band and the states are the engine's. It starts at 50/50.
- **S — E10's supertrend schemes S1–S4 on the same three pairs.** The desk's supertrend runs on each leg, and four weight
  schemes cover the four buy/sell combinations:
  - S1 rotate, cash if both sell;
  - S2 rotate, always invested;
  - S3 tilt 75/25;
  - S4 each leg on its own.
- **M — E10's momentum rules on the same pairs** (12-1, 12-month, 6-month): hold the leg with the higher momentum.
- **B — E11's India book:** N100, MC100, SC100 and gold; neutral = 50% gold and 16.7% per index. The versions are:
  - ST-EQ, ST-BR, ST-TL;
  - ST-MO-12-1, ST-MO-12, ST-MO-6;
  - GR-12-1, GR-12, GR-6;
  - MO-12-1, MO-12, MO-6;
  - REF-S2 (SC100 + gold).

**With the switch, each version starts from its starting weights with the switch applied** (gold's part in the liquid ETF
if gold is SELL that week).

## Measures, null and robustness

**Measures**, for each strategy as before and with the switch:
- CAGR, maximum drawdown and volatility;
- the differences between the two;
- the share of weeks the switch holds the liquid ETF;
- the share of weeks the old strategy held gold while it was SELL;
- switches a year.

**Benchmarks:** each pair's 50/50 and the book's neutral bought once, gold alone, each index alone, and the liquid ETF
alone.

**Null N-G:** the same strategy and the same switch, but gold's SELL weeks slid in time. Every circular shift of gold's
weekly state, from 26 weeks to the period's length less 26, through the same book. The switch's CAGR and drawdown are
placed among the shifted switches (≤ 5 or ≥ 95 notable). It asks whether gold's actual SELL timing helps, rather than any
SELL periods of the same total length.

**Robustness:** the switch with LiquidCase instead of Liquid (CAGR only).

## Checks (a failure stops the run)

- Each frame's closes equal the loader's.
- The as-before Rule R through the book of this study equals the engine's book on each pair.
- Every target row sums to 1, as before and with the switch.
- The switch never puts weight in gold on a SELL week, and never moves weight on a BUY week.
- Gold's supertrend states, Rule R's states and the book's signals, recomputed on histories cut at 8 decision weeks, are
  unchanged.
- Every N-G shift keeps gold's number of SELL weeks.

## Added 2026-11-26, after the first run — the pairs' start

**What went wrong.** The engine's `pair_frame` fixes every gold pair's start at 2015-01-12, the first day of the earlier
gold file. In the first run the three gold pairs (families R, S and M) therefore began on 2015-01-16, against this design.
They also had no warm-up year: Rule R's band and the 12-month momentum had no history before that first decision. The
book (family B) was not affected; it began on 2012-01-06 as designed.

**The fix.** The script now builds the engine's pair frame itself, with the same construction, from 2011-01-01 as
designed. A new check confirms it equals the engine's frame when both start on 2015-01-12. Nothing else changes.
