**Status:** EXPLORATORY — description of the past, not a finding, not a test of any rule. All four series have been
studied before (E08–E11), so nothing here can serve as an unseen test.
**Asked:** 2026-11-26 — "PDBC, SDCI, and COM seem to have a longer history and some sort of information with respect to
the SPY. Just get the OHLCV of these ETFs from the DB, and try to calculate the super trend here with respect to SPY also.
Check whether we can have a portfolio of these four with some rotation. Also, document the +1, -1 standard deviation as
well as the 50/50 plot."
**Script:** `python explore/E12_cmdty_rotation/cmdty.py [--draws 500]` → `explore/E12_cmdty_rotation/out/`
**Data:** `analysis/approved_data.load_us_etf()`: SPY, PDBC, SDCI and COM (total return, rule H4; each fund from its start
under rule H5: SPY 2010-01-04, PDBC 2016-01-04, COM 2019-01-02, SDCI 2020-01-02), to 2026-11-18.

## OHLCV: what the research database holds

Checked on 2026-11-26, before this design:
- **Refinitiv Eikon table (`ohlcv_eikon_us`, the approved files' source).** It has no open, high or low for any of the four
  before 2026, and has them on only 22% of 2026's rows (F-RA-D13).
- **EODHD table (`us_ohlcv_eodhd`).** It has full OHLCV for SPY and PDBC to 2026-07-31, but nothing for SDCI or COM.

Real weekly highs and lows therefore exist for at most two of the four assets. So every supertrend here uses weekly bars
built from closes (high = the week's highest close, low = its lowest), as E10 and E11 did for US funds. That keeps the
four on one footing. Trades fill at the next session's close (rule H3).

## Part 1 — the three pairs (ETF ÷ SPY)

Each pair is the engine's pair frame (`pair_frame(px, "X|SPY", "plain")`), as in E10. It runs from the 53rd decision week
of its own history:
- PDBC ÷ SPY from 2017-01-06;
- COM ÷ SPY from 2020-01-03;
- SDCI ÷ SPY from 2020-12-31.

For each pair:

1. **The ±1 sd band.** The plain ratio X ÷ SPY at each decision week, its trailing 52-week mean, and mean ± 1 sd (the band
   of T02/T03). The chart marks the trades of **Rule R**, T03's rule:
   - the first week the ratio is below mean − 1 sd: hold X (the leg that has lagged) until the ratio is back at the mean;
   - above mean + 1 sd: hold SPY until it is back at the mean;
   - otherwise 50/50.
2. **The 50/50.** The value of 1 for:
   - Rule R;
   - the relative supertrend rule (item 3);
   - 50/50 bought once and never rebalanced (the engine's comparator);
   - X alone;
   - SPY alone.
   All after costs, with their drawdowns.
3. **The supertrend against SPY.** The desk's Weekly LW supertrend (E10's functions, checked there against the desk's code)
   runs:
   - on X and on SPY, each on its own weekly bars (the absolute signals);
   - on the ratio X ÷ SPY, on weekly bars of the daily ratio on the pair's calendar (the relative signal).

   **Rule RS:** hold X while the ratio's supertrend is BUY, SPY while it is SELL.
4. **Nulls.**
   - Rule R against N-A, as in T03 and E10: 500 draws with E10's seeds. Its numbers must equal E10's R52 row, which is a
     check.
   - Rule RS against N-S, as E10's momentum and supertrend: its BUY and SELL spells in a random order, 500 draws, seed
     `default_rng([0, E10 pair id, 5, draw])`.

## Part 2 — the four-asset rotation book

The four assets run on their common calendar from 2020-01-02 (SDCI's start under H5). Decisions fall on the last trading
day of each ISO week. Every strategy starts on the 53rd decision week (the 12-month lookback needs 52), at the neutral
weights, and then follows its weekly targets:
- trades at the next session's fill (the close, H3);
- 10.4 bps per side on value traded;
- units held between changes;
- always invested, no cash.

The book is E11's (`nbook`), which E11 checked against the engine.

**Classes and neutral:**
- equity = SPY, base 50%;
- commodity = PDBC, SDCI and COM, base 50%;
- neutral = SPY 50% and each commodity ETF 16.7%.

**Signals at each decision day (all point in time):**
- **Absolute supertrend:** BUY or SELL for each of the four assets, as in E11.
- **Relative supertrend:** BUY or SELL for each commodity ETF, from its ratio to SPY (Part 1's relative signal, read at
  the book's decision days).
- **Rule R state:** for each pair (X, SPY, or 50/50), read at the book's decision days from the pair's latest decision.
- **12-month momentum:** of each asset, on the decision-week closes.

**The strategies (fixed before the run):**

1. **ST-EQ, ST-BR, ST-TL** — E11's class rules on the absolute supertrend (equal split, breadth, tilt):
   - a class is on when a member is BUY;
   - an off class hands its money to the on class (ST-TL keeps half);
   - nothing on → neutral.
2. **ST-MO-12** — as ST-EQ, but the commodity money goes to the BUY commodity ETF with the highest 12-month momentum.
3. **GR-12** — gate and rank: 100% in the BUY asset with the highest 12-month momentum; nothing BUY → neutral.
4. **MO-12** — momentum only: 100% in the asset with the highest 12-month momentum.
5. **RS-TL** — relative tilt: from neutral, each commodity ETF whose ratio to SPY has a SELL supertrend hands its 16.7% to
   SPY.
6. **RS-ROT** — relative rotation:
   - the commodity ETFs whose ratio supertrend is BUY share 100% equally;
   - none → 100% SPY.

   With one ETF this is Rule RS.
7. **RR-3** — Rule R sleeves:
   - a third of the book follows each pair's Rule R state (X, SPY or half each);
   - with every pair at 50/50 the book is neutral.

**Benchmarks**, each bought once on the same day:
- neutral;
- 25% each;
- each asset alone.

**Null N-T:** every circular shift of the weekly signal panel (all signals of all assets together, so their agreement is
kept), from 26 weeks to the period's length less 26, through the same book. Percentiles of CAGR and maximum drawdown are
notable at ≤ 5 or ≥ 95.

## Checks (a failure stops the run)

- The pair frames' and the book frame's closes equal the loader's.
- Rule R on each pair:
  - through the general book equals the engine's book;
  - its CAGR, switches and N-A percentile equal E10's R52 row.
- The band: (ratio − mean) ÷ sd equals the z that Rule R uses, on every banded week.
- The book's signals and every strategy's targets, recomputed on histories cut at 8 decision weeks, are unchanged.
- Weight rows sum to 1.
- Every N-T shift keeps each asset's BUY weeks and each pair's Rule R state counts (measured).
- Every N-S draw keeps Rule RS's spell counts.

## What would make the rotation interesting, and the limits

**What would make it interesting:** a strategy ahead of neutral and of SPY with a notable N-T percentile. Even then this is
description: about 5.9 years (2021-01 → 2026-11), nine strategies, and seen data. A rule worth keeping would need its own
locked test.

**Limits:**
- SDCI sets the book's start, so the book misses 2020 (the COVID crash) and covers one commodity cycle (the 2021–2022
  inflation surge and its unwind).
- PDBC's pair is the longest, from 2017.
- Closes only, so no intraday highs or lows in the supertrend.
- No cash.
