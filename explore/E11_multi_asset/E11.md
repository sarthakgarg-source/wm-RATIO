# E11 — Four assets: the desk's supertrend for equity against gold, momentum for which index (EXPLORATORY)

**Status:** EXPLORATORY — description of the past, not a finding, not a test of any rule. All four series have been
studied before (T02/T03, E01–E10), so nothing here can serve as an unseen test.
**Asked:** 2026-11-25 — "now can you extend it to include more than 2 constituents, for eg. Gold, Nifty 100 , MC100,
SC100. MOST of the time indices will be in sync, meaning they will agree on the signal . but how can we design this
strategy". The desk's answer to the design proposed in reply: "lets keep both supertrend and momentum and all the
versions of it. lets go". So every version below is run, with no cash and without Micro250.
**Script:** `python explore/E11_multi_asset/multi.py [--max-shifts N]` → `explore/E11_multi_asset/out/`
**Data:** `analysis/approved_data.load_daily()`: N100, MC100 and SC100 (NSE price indices) and MCX gold futures
(rupees), on the four assets' common calendar from 2015-01-12 (gold's first day) to 2026-08-28.

## Why two decisions

A side check made before this design (the desk's supertrend, 2016-01 → 2026-08) found:
- the three equity indices on the same signal in 79% of weeks;
- gold on a different signal from the equity majority in 57% of weeks;
- weekly returns correlating 0.80–0.94 among the indices and about 0 with gold.

So the four assets are close to two: the equity block and gold. The design makes two decisions:
- **equity against gold**, by the supertrend, where the signals differ;
- **which index**, by momentum (relative strength), because the indices' own trends mostly agree.

§1 recomputes these measures in the script.

## The signals and the conventions

- **Supertrend:** the desk's Weekly LW supertrend on each asset's own weekly bars, read at each decision day: BUY or SELL
  per asset. It uses E10's functions (`explore/E10_trend_rotation/rotation.py`: desk_bars, desk_supertrend,
  leg_state_at), which E10 checked against the desk's own code.
- **Momentum:** on the decision-week closes of the four-asset calendar:
  - 12-1 = the return from 52 weeks back to 4 weeks back;
  - 12-month = the last 52 weeks;
  - 6-month = the last 26 weeks.
- **Decisions and trades:** decisions on the last trading day of each ISO week of the common calendar; trades at the
  next session's fill (OHLC4); 10.4 bps per side on value traded; units held between changes; daily NAV.
- **Common start:** the 53rd decision week (the 12-month lookback needs 52), at the neutral weights. From the second
  decision each strategy follows its targets, trading only when its target weights change.
- **Neutral (resting) weights:** 50% gold and 16.7% in each index. They are used at the start and whenever a version has
  nothing on.

## The strategies (fixed before the run; every one always invested, no cash)

1. **ST-EQ — supertrend, equal split.**
   - The equity half is split equally among the indices whose supertrend is BUY.
   - Gold's half is in gold if gold is BUY.
   - A side with nothing on hands its half to the other side, if that side has something on.

   | equity indices BUY | gold | weights |
   |---|---|---|
   | one or more | BUY | 50% gold; 50% split equally among the BUY indices |
   | one or more | SELL | 100% split equally among the BUY indices |
   | none | BUY | 100% gold |
   | none | SELL | neutral |

   With one index this is E10's S2.
2. **ST-MO-12-1, ST-MO-12, ST-MO-6 — supertrend plus momentum pick.** As ST-EQ, but the equity money goes to the single
   BUY index with the highest momentum. Ties go to the larger index (N100, then MC100, then SC100).
3. **ST-BR — supertrend, breadth.** The equity money is always spread equally over the three indices; its size follows
   how many of them are BUY (k):

   | k | gold | equity (equal over all three) | gold |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 3 / 2 / 1 / 0 | BUY | 50% / 33.3% / 16.7% / 0% | the rest |
   | 1, 2 or 3 | SELL | 100% | 0% |
   | 0 | SELL | neutral | |

4. **GR-12-1, GR-12, GR-6 — gate and rank.** This is the desk's rotation design (wm-IDXrot) with one slot:
   - among the four assets whose supertrend is BUY, hold 100% of the one with the highest momentum;
   - if none is BUY, neutral.
5. **MO-12-1, MO-12, MO-6 — momentum only.** Hold 100% of the asset with the highest momentum among the four. This is
   E10's pair momentum extended to four assets.

**Reference:** E10's S2 on SC100 and gold alone (the desk's supertrend on each), booked on the same days. It must equal
E10's result.

**Benchmarks:** neutral bought once (50% gold, 16.7% each index); 25% each bought once; each asset bought once.

## Against chance

The signal panel is slid in time against the prices by every shift from 26 weeks to the length of the period less 26
weeks. The shifts are exhaustive and circular, so every asset keeps its BUY weeks and the indices keep their agreement.
- **N-T (timing):** the four supertrend states and the momentum scores are slid together. This measures what the
  whole timing is worth (every strategy and the reference).
- **N-P (pick):** the supertrend states stay real and only the momentum scores are slid. This measures what picking by
  momentum is worth, given the gates (ST-MO and GR).

For each, the percentile of the real CAGR and of the real max drawdown among the shifts; ≤ 5 or ≥ 95 = notable. Each
shift's per-asset BUY weeks and the indices' agreement are measured.

## Checks

- The four-asset frame's closes equal the loader's, and its calendar equals the SC100 ÷ gold pair frame's.
- The book (any number of assets) equals the engine's book on a two-asset run (Rule R on SC100 ÷ gold), and reproduces
  E10's S2 on SC100 ÷ gold.
- Point in time: supertrend states, momentum and every strategy's targets, recomputed on the history cut at 8 dates,
  equal the full-history values up to each cut.
- Every weight row sums to 1.

## Limits

- About 10.6 years (2016–2026), one regime; the equity block turned only about ten times each way.
- Price indices (no dividends) against gold futures; no cash.
- Eleven strategies (and the reference) on seen data: the more versions, the more chances that one looks good by luck.
  All versions and their nulls are reported together.

## How to read it (2026-11-25)

Everything above was written before the first run and is unchanged. A run with 20 shifts checked the code; the full run
used every shift (504 shifts, of 26–529 weeks). One display fix followed the full run: the strategy codes are written
12-1 as in this design (not 12_1), and the rules table is aligned left. Every number and every null draw was identical
after it.

Checks:
- the four-asset calendar equals the SC100 ÷ gold pair frame's;
- the book equals the engine's on Rule R (max NAV difference 6.7e-16) and reproduces E10's S2 on SC100 ÷ gold exactly;
- 8 history cuts gave 0 violations, and every weight row sums to 1;
- all 504 shifts kept each asset's BUY weeks and the indices' agreement;
- a probe of every page state (12 states, 2,198 values) found 0 mismatches against the CSVs.

Every number is in `out/E11_results.md` and on the page; all run from 2016-01-15 to 2026-08-28.

1. **The four assets really are two** (555 weeks).
   - The three equity indices give the same supertrend signal in 79% of weeks; gold differs from the equity majority in
     57%.
   - Weekly returns correlate +0.80 to +0.94 among the indices and −0.02 to −0.04 with gold.
   - No index reliably leads the turns. The equity block turned to SELL 10 times: SC100 was among the first to flip in 8
     of them, but alone first in only 2. It turned to BUY 11 times: N100 was among the first in 8, alone in 2.
2. **Benchmarks.** Neutral bought once (50% gold, 16.7% each index) made +16.6% a year with a max drawdown of −21%;
   25% each +15.6% (−34%); N100 +12.1%, MC100 +16.8%, SC100 +14.2%, gold +18.4% (−21%).
3. **The supertrend's equity-against-gold decision is what works.**
   - ST-EQ (equal split) made +21.3% a year (+4.69 pp over neutral); ST-BR (breadth) +22.5% (+5.84 pp).
   - Both beat every single asset, with a max drawdown of −19% against neutral's −21% and 25% each's −34%.
   - Both are notable against the timing null: percentiles 96.6 and 97.6.
4. **Picking the index by momentum adds nothing.**
   - ST-MO made +20.8% to +20.9% with every lookback, slightly below the equal split.
   - Against momentum slid in time with the supertrend kept (N-P), its percentiles are 62.7–71.0.
   - Its timing percentiles (92.9–94.4) fall just short of notable; its drawdown percentiles (97.8–98.4) are notable.
   - It puts everything in one asset in 52% of weeks.
5. **Gate and rank earns more but falls further.**
   - GR made +22.8% to +24.3% a year, with max drawdowns of −26% to −32%, and is all in one asset in 93% of weeks.
   - Timing percentiles 91.7–95.2 (GR-6 notable at 95.2); N-P 67.3–75.2.
6. **Momentum alone is the weakest:** MO made +16.3% to +19.9%, with max drawdowns of −29% to −42% and timing
   percentiles of 62.5–82.7.
7. **The two-asset reference is still the best.**
   - SC100 + gold alone (E10's S2) made +25.7% a year with a max drawdown of −19%; timing percentile 99.2.
   - Adding N100 and MC100 diluted SC100's lead instead of adding to it.
   - But SC100 was the best of the three gold pairs E10 looked at, so part of this lead is hindsight. The four-asset
     ST-EQ and ST-BR pick no index in advance.
8. **Plain reading.**
   - With more than two assets the result comes from the equity-against-gold decision made by the desk's supertrend.
   - How the equity money is spread matters little: equal or breadth spreading did slightly better than picking by
     momentum. Concentrating in one asset (gate and rank) adds both return and drawdown.
   - Three of the eleven versions are notable against the timing null (ST-EQ, ST-BR, GR-6), and they share most of their
     signals.
   - Seen data, one decade: a candidate for a pre-registered test, not a finding.

## Added on 2026-11-25, before its run: the US book, and a tilt version for both books

**Asked:** 2026-11-25 — "we need to add us asset clases as well the complete idea in that is it should go into equity
and commodity and gold and should have mix of both", then "we need to keep us assets together and indian assets
together not a mix". So a second, separate book of US assets, in dollars, runs with the same rules. The Indian book is
unchanged, and nothing mixes the two (no currency conversion is needed).

- **The US book.**
  - Members: equity = SPY (large caps) and IJR (small caps); commodity = GCC (WisdomTree Enhanced Commodity); gold = GLD.
  - GCC is the only broad commodity ETF with approved history from 2010; the others start in 2015–2020.
  - All four run from 2010-01-04 on the same days (total return, fills at the close), read through `load_us_etf()`,
    `load_us_small()` and `load_us_gold()`.
  - The book runs from its 53rd decision week (2011-01) to 2026-11-18.
  - The supertrend runs on closes-only weekly bars, as on E10's US legs.
- **Classes and base weights.**
  - US: equity 50% (each member half of it), commodity 25%, gold 25%, so the US neutral is 25% in each asset.
  - India stays as above: equity 50% (16.7% per index) and gold 50%.
- **The rules, written for any number of classes.** They reduce exactly to the Indian rules above.
  - A class is on when any of its members' supertrends is BUY. In the breadth version it is on in proportion to its BUY
    members.
  - A class's money goes to its BUY members: equally (ST-EQ), to the one with the highest momentum (ST-MO), or equally
    over all its members (ST-BR).
  - The money of a class that is off (in breadth, the off part) goes to the other classes that are on, in proportion to
    their base weights.
  - If no other class is on, that money stays in its own class (breadth). If nothing is on at all, the book is neutral.
  - GR and MO work on the four assets regardless of class, as above.
- **A new version for both books, ST-TL — supertrend, tilt** (the desk asked for a mix):
  - a class that is off keeps half of its base weight, spread equally over its members;
  - only the other half goes to the classes that are on;
  - with two classes this is E10's S3 (75/25).

  It is added to the Indian book too. The Indian book's other results must not change; this is checked against the first
  run's CSVs.
- **US reference:** E10's S2 on IJR and GLD alone (IJR ÷ GLD, E10's US version of SC100 ÷ gold), booked on the same
  days. It must equal E10's result.
- **Check (no nulls):** the US book with PDBC (Invesco Optimum Yield Diversified Commodity, from 2016) as the commodity,
  from its own 53rd decision week (2017-01).
- Nulls, checks, costs and outputs as above, for each book. The CSVs gain a `book` column; the weights and signals files
  become long (one row per book, strategy or asset, and week).

The block above was written before this run and is unchanged. A run with 20 shifts checked the code; the full run used
every shift (India 504 of 26–529 weeks, US 778 of 26–803 weeks). One display fix followed (the breadth chart's labels

1. **The tilt in India keeps the gain and cuts the fall** (2016-01-15 → 2026-08-28).
   - ST-TL made +19.4% a year (+2.75 pp over neutral), with volatility of 12% and a max drawdown of −17%, the smallest of
     any Indian strategy (neutral −21%).
   - It is never all in one asset, and it is notable against the timing null (96.2).
   - India now has four versions notable on timing: ST-EQ, ST-BR, ST-TL and GR-6.
2. **The US assets are less in sync between classes, and the equity pair flips more** (2011-01-07 → 2026-11-18,
   778 weeks).
   - SPY and IJR give the same signal in 80% of weeks. GCC differs from the equity pair in 40% of weeks, GLD in 47%.
   - Weekly returns: SPY–IJR +0.86, the equity ETFs with GCC +0.40 to +0.41 and with GLD +0.10 to +0.13, GCC with GLD
     +0.47.
   - The supertrends flip 3.8–4.7 times a year (India 2.4–2.9), on closes-only bars.
3. **US benchmarks.** Neutral (25% each) made +9.2% a year with a max drawdown of −31%. SPY made +13.6% (−34%), IJR
   +10.7% (−44%), GCC +0.7% (−62%), GLD +6.1% (−46%).
4. **In the US no version beat the fixed 25% mix.**
   - All twelve trail neutral, by 0.70 pp (MO-6, +8.5%) to 5.59 pp (MO-12-1, +3.6%).
   - The supertrend versions made +6.5% (ST-BR) to +7.7% (ST-TL), with timing percentiles of 40.7–52.7, no better than
     the same signals at random times. ST-TL's max drawdown (−24%) is smaller than neutral's.
   - Picking SPY or IJR by 12-month momentum was notably worse than a random-time ranking (N-P 1.0).
   - The reference, IJR + GLD alone, made +8.4% (timing 57.8). SPY alone beat every mix.
5. **With PDBC as the commodity** (2017-01-06 → 2026-11-18, neutral +11.1% a year; the check):
   - the supertrend versions are about level with neutral (ST-EQ, ST-BR, ST-TL +0.37 to +0.47 pp);
   - gate and rank is ahead (GR-12-1 +3.46 pp, GR-12 +2.56 pp);
   - the others trail.

   So the US result moves with the commodity fund and the period, but no version shows a clear edge in either.
6. **Plain reading.**
   - The same rules that worked in India did nothing in the US. India's edge sits in its equity-against-gold switch,
     where the two move independently and the supertrend separated good weeks from bad.
   - In the US the equity supertrend kept selling before rebounds (as in E10), GCC lost money for a decade, and SPY alone
     outran every mix.
   - The design is not a general edge; at most it is a market-specific one, still on seen data.
