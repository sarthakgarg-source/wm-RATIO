## 1. The same-period comparison (description)

SC100 ÷ gold (MCX, rupees) and SPY ÷ GLD (dollars) on their common ISO weeks from 2015-01-12 (the first day of the gold
pairs) to 2026-08-28, weekly closes on each pair's decision days. Charts: the four legs and the two ratios as the value
of 1 from the first common week; the 52-week z of both; the rolling 52-week correlation of their weekly changes.
Measures: the correlation of weekly and of 4-week log changes of the two ratios; the correlation of their 52-week z; the
share of weeks with z on the same side of 0 and both beyond ±1 the same way; the share of weeks Rule R (52 weeks) holds
the same kind of asset in both (equity, gold or 50/50); the legs' weekly correlations (SC100 with SPY, MCX gold with
GLD); each leg's CAGR. The same measures for N100 ÷ gold and MC100 ÷ gold against SPY ÷ GLD, in the table only.

## 2–5. The rules

All decisions on the last trading day of each ISO week of the pair's common calendar; trades fill the next session at
`fill` (OHLC4 for NSE/MCX, the close for US ETFs); 10.4 bps per side on value traded; units held between trades; daily
NAV. Every rule on a pair starts on the same day: the first decision week at which all of them have a signal (the 53rd
decision week of the pair, since the 12-month lookback needs 52 weeks). From that day each rule starts at 50/50 by value
on the next session, then follows its weekly targets (the engine's convention).

- **Rule R** (T03's rule, 52-week window): z of the plain ratio a ÷ b on the last 52 weekly closes; from 50/50 hold a
  on the first week z < −1, b on the first week z > +1; back to 50/50 when z returns through 0; no maximum hold.
  States from `explore/E01_ratio_cycles/variants.rule_states`.
- **Momentum** (relative, "simple momentum"): hold the leg with the higher momentum, measured on decision-week closes:
  **12-1** = the return from 52 weeks back to 4 weeks back; **12-month** = the last 52 weeks; **6-month** = the last
  26 weeks. (Holding the leg with the higher return is the same as following the sign of the ratio's own momentum.)
- **Supertrend** on each leg's weekly bars, the standard settings, ATR 10 and multiplier 3 (fixed here, not tuned):
  weekly high = the highest daily high of the week's common days, low = the lowest low, close = the decision day's
  close; true range on the previous weekly close; ATR = Wilder's average (seeded with the simple mean of the first 10);
  bands at (high + low) ÷ 2 ± 3 × ATR, carried with the usual ratchet; **buy** while the close is above the trailing
  band, **sell** after a close below it. The first direction is sell until the first close above the upper band (all
  rules start a year later). Indian legs use their daily highs and lows (SC100 has none before 2011-04, so its early
  bars are built from closes). US series have closes only, so their weekly high and low are the highest and lowest daily
  close. Check: the Indian legs' states with closes-only bars, reported as agreement.
- **The four combinations and their weights** (first leg / second leg / cash; cash earns 0%: the approved data has no
  cash series):

  | first leg | second leg | S1 rotate, cash if both sell | S2 rotate, always invested | S3 tilt | S4 each leg on its own |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | buy | buy | 50 / 50 / 0 | 50 / 50 / 0 | 50 / 50 / 0 | 50 / 50 / 0 |
  | buy | sell | 100 / 0 / 0 | 100 / 0 / 0 | 75 / 25 / 0 | 50 / 0 / 50 |
  | sell | buy | 0 / 100 / 0 | 0 / 100 / 0 | 25 / 75 / 0 | 0 / 50 / 50 |
  | sell | sell | 0 / 0 / 100 | 50 / 50 / 0 | 50 / 50 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 100 |

  The sleeve trades only when the combination changes, back to the scheme's weights by value; otherwise units are held.
- **Benchmarks:** 50/50 bought once (the house benchmark), each leg bought once.

**The book.** One book for every rule: a target of weights (first leg, second leg, cash) per decision week; on a change,
it rebalances at the next session's fills, selling before buying, 10.4 bps per side on value traded. For targets that
are only "all a", "all b" or "50/50" this is the engine's book (`analysis.ratio_engine.book`); the script checks the NAV
equals the engine's on every Rule R and momentum run.

**Measures** per pair and rule, from the common start to the pair's last day: CAGR; excess over 50/50 bought once and
over each leg; volatility; max drawdown; switches a year; weeks in each state. Supertrend also: how often each
combination occurs, and each leg's average next-week return in its buy weeks and in its sell weeks.

## 3. Band touches against z signals

For every pair, 52-week window:
- **The same closes:** the band test (r < mean − sd, r > mean + sd) against the z test (z < −1, z > +1) week by week,
  and the T02/T03 engine's states (`ratio_engine.decide`, the band) against the z-based states (`rule_states`).
- **Daily closes against the weekly band:** each day's ratio against the band known that day (the last decision week's
  mean ± sd). Of the weekly signals (the first week the close is beyond the band), the share that a daily close had
  touched the same side during that week, and the share touched on an earlier day of the week; of the daily first
  touches, the share whose week closed beyond the band.
- **Weekly 52-week z against daily 250-day z** (both about a year): the share of Rule R's 52-week entries with a
  250-day entry the same way within two weeks, and the share of weeks in the same state.

## Against chance

The **same spells in a random order (N-S)**: a two-state series (momentum's legs; each leg's supertrend buy/sell) is
cut into spells; the spells of each state are permuted among that state's positions, so the number of switches, the
spells and the weeks in each state are kept exactly. Supertrend shuffles each leg's series on its own and rebuilds the
combination (its counts are measured, not promised). Rule R uses T03's null N-A (`ratio_engine.null_A`). 500 draws per
pair and rule, through the same book; seed `default_rng([market_id (0 US, 1 India), pair_id, rule_id, draw])`, rule_id
0 Rule R, 1–3 momentum 12-1 / 12 / 6, 4 supertrend (one shuffle per draw shared by S1–S4). Percentile of the real CAGR
and of the real max drawdown among the draws; ≤ 5 or ≥ 95 = notable. Every draw's counts are measured.

## Checks

- The book equals the engine's book on every Rule R and momentum run (NAV, max abs difference < 1e-9).
- Rule R with the 52-week window equals T03 on the Indian pairs (`variants.check_against_t03`).
- Point in time: z, momentum and both legs' supertrend recomputed on the history cut at 8 dates per pair equal the
  full-history values up to each cut.
- Pair closes equal the loaders'; every null draw's counts are measured.

## Limits

- NSE indices are price indices (no dividends) and MCX gold is futures in rupees; US series are total return in dollars.
- Cash earns 0% (no approved cash series); S1 and S4 are understated by about the cash rate times their time in cash.
- One setting each for supertrend (10, 3) and three lookbacks for momentum, fixed here; nothing is tuned.
- Weekly decisions only; one regime (2007–2026 India, 2011–2026 US); the commodity ETFs move together.

- **The US pairs are unchanged:** they keep the engine's own start and the same data.
- **The similarity window (section 1)** follows the data: 2011-01-01 to 2026-11-20. It was 2015-01-12 to 2026-08-28,
  bounded then by gold's first day and India's last.
- **The switch, on the Indian gold pairs (N100, MC100 and SC100 against gold).** It follows E13's definition:
  - the book's third holding is the liquid ETF, which replaces cash. It is bought and sold at its level at 10.4 bps per
    side, like the legs (`variants.wbook`'s cost rule);
  - in any week whose decision falls in gold's weekly supertrend SELL, gold's weight moves to the liquid ETF;
  - the start is 50/50, with gold's half in the liquid ETF if gold is SELL at the first decision.

  This applies to every rule (Rule R, the three momentum rules and the four supertrend schemes) and to every null draw.
  Gold's SELL weeks are fixed by the calendar, so they stay the same in every draw. Gold's supertrend for the switch is
  the same series as the gold leg's supertrend in the schemes. So in the supertrend null on a gold pair, the shuffled
  gold spells decide the scheme's weights, but the real SELL weeks still move any gold held to the liquid ETF.
- **The Indian index pairs are unchanged in rules:** cash still earns 0 in S1 and S4, as in this study's design. The
  switch concerns gold only.
- **Reporting:** the average weights are the weights as held, so on the gold pairs the third is the liquid ETF's share.
  Rule R's trades on a gold pair are the state changes. A gold-held trade's extra is (gold or the liquid ETF, as held) −
  the peer.
- **Checks:**
  - the switched book must equal `variants.book3` (the same switch, written separately) for Rule R and momentum on every
    gold pair, to 1e-9;
  - gbook without the switch must still equal the engine's book;
  - Rule R's decisions must equal the engine's (the check against T03's evidence becomes a check against the engine).
- **Seeds, draws, the supertrend and the schemes** are unchanged.
