## 1. Idea and references            (written BEFORE the run)

- **The idea in one sentence:** the idea of T01 — on an NSE index pair, after the ratio of the two legs' prices has moved more than one standard deviation away from its trailing-year average it drifts back, so holding only the lagging leg from the first week beyond ±1 sd until the ratio is back at its mean earns a higher after-cost CAGR and a shallower drawdown than the same trades at random moments — with the band drawn on the **plain price ratio P_a ÷ P_b**, the number on the ratio chart, instead of on its logarithm.
- **Where it came from:** the request of 2026-11-13, after T01: "we do not want to take log ratio change it to normal ratio" (tests/T02/PLAN.md). T01's own brief (tests/T01/PLAN.md) spoke of "the ratio of a pair against its trailing mean ± 1 sd band"; the logarithm was a choice made in T01 §3 step 4, not part of the brief.
- **The decision this will change:** whether T01's verdict (REFUTED, F-RA-S01) belongs to the ratio-band idea or only to its log form. If T02 also fails, the ratio-band switch is closed in both forms and the sleeve work stays on the supertrend / gold switch. If T02 passes where T01 failed, the pass rests on the second form tried on the same window and is PROVISIONAL at most (see Multiplicity and the outcome map).
- **What is already known:**

| ref | what it says | relationship |
|---|---|---|
| T01 F-RA-S01 (REFUTED), 2026-11-12 | the same rule on ln(P_a ÷ P_b): C1 0 of 6, C2 2 of 6, C3 0 of 6; CAGR below the N-A median in 5 of 6 index pairs; the best of six settings per pair at the 29.4th–77.2nd percentile of N-A; MDE 2.6–11.4 pp/yr | the test this one repeats with one ingredient changed |
| T01 F-RA-S02 (PROVISIONAL) | per trade, the held leg beat its peer more often than not (positive median) but the mean and the sum of the extra were negative: a few large losers | expected to carry over, since most trades should be the same |
| T01 F-RA-M01 (FIXED) | the timing null must permute blocks of abutting spells, not single spells, or the switch count is not held | written into this design from the start (§2 null table, §3 step 9) |
| arithmetic of the two forms | ln is increasing, so both forms order the weeks the same way; they differ only in where the trailing mean and sd fall. Where the ratio's range inside the window is small against its level, ln is close to a straight line over that range and the two bands nearly coincide; the gap grows with that range (the 2008 and 2020 falls, the gold pairs, and in general the longer window) | why T02 is expected to make mostly the same decisions as T01, and where it can differ |
| orientation | ln(P_b ÷ P_a) = −ln(P_a ÷ P_b), so T01's band was the same whichever leg was on top; the plain ratio is not symmetric (the mean of 1/x is not 1 ÷ the mean of x), so a ÷ b and b ÷ a can touch their bands in different weeks | T02 fixes the orientation as T01's (§3 step 4) and does not test the reverse |

- **What this test is NOT asking:** whether the log or the plain form is "right" in general; whether the reverse orientation (b ÷ a) gives a different answer; any other transform of the ratio (a z-score, the percentage distance from the mean, a volatility-scaled band); a long/short book; anything T01 did not ask.

## 2. Hypothesis and pre-registered criteria   (BEFORE)

- **What I already saw:** all of T01: the log form of this rule fails every criterion at every window and exit, and the per-trade pattern is a positive median with a negative mean. Before this design was locked, the plain-ratio code path was executed with 0 draws to check that the engine runs and that the independent verifier agrees with it (the engine three times, while finding a console-encoding error in its final print; the verifier twice, once with the right form and once with the wrong one); the output was discarded and only exit codes and the error's last line were read. No number of the plain-ratio rule (no decision, round trip, CAGR or drawdown) has been read. Also before the lock, on 2026-11-13, the exploratory study E01 (explore/E01_ratio_cycles/, EXPLORATORY, design in E01.md) charted every pair's plain ratio with its trailing-52-week mean ± 1 sd — the band of this test, drawn but not traded — and compared the ratios' weekly changes with 2,000 sign-flipped random walks per pair, first with the drift removed over the whole history and then, the same day, point-in-time (drift and threshold from earlier weeks only). What it showed, in both versions: no index pair's spectrum differs from a random walk's overall (no dominant cycle length; in the point-in-time version only Micro250 ÷ GoldMCX does, on 3½ years); at the 13–52-week horizons this rule trades on, no index pair's ratio reverses more than a random walk's — the variance ratios are near or above 1, MC100 ÷ SC100 continues at 8–13 weeks and the gold pairs continue at most horizons; no tested pair has an overall month-of-year pattern, though April (the smaller-cap leg ahead) is notable in the three 20-year index pairs; swing lengths are no more regular than a random walk's. On 2026-11-16, also before the lock, the exploratory study E02 (explore/E02_ratio_segments/, EXPLORATORY and HINDSIGHT, design in E02.md) segmented every pair's daily plain ratio into up / down / sideways stretches between prominent peaks and troughs (the desk's market-segregation method, trend in % a year). What it showed: long stretches in both directions in every pair (for N100 ÷ SC100 at the reference setting about 41% of the time up, 39% down, 21% sideways), turning points that mostly coincide with E01's zig-zag, and the code's own ±30° test calling every segment sideways. On 2026-11-17, also before the lock, E01 added the z-score of the ratio, (ratio − trailing 52-week mean) ÷ trailing sd, which is this test's band in units of its own sd (z = ±1 is the band's edge), described against the same random walks: the share of weeks beyond ±1 and ±2, the length of stretches beyond ±1, and the z at E01's turning points. Nothing about what follows a band touch was computed (no return, no time back to the mean, no hit rate), and of the engine only the band's mean and sd were read, to check that the z is the band. What it showed: a trailing 52-week z is beyond ±1 in more than half the weeks even on a random walk; the 20-year index pairs spend about as long outside the band as the random walks do; and their turning points fall near z = ±2, as the random walks' do. The one index pair that differs on time outside the band, SC100 ÷ Micro250, is the one where the drift check finds the random-walk comparison unreliable. None of this is a T02 statistic or changes T02's design; it agrees with the expectation stated below. The expected result, given T01 and the arithmetic above, is that H1 and H2 fail again with percentiles close to T01's; the test is written so that it can pass.
- **Hypotheses:**
  - **H1 (timing):** with the band on P_a ÷ P_b, the rule's after-cost CAGR beats random timing with the same switches and exposure (null N-A) at the 95th percentile, and its max drawdown is shallower than N-A's median, in at least 4 of the 6 index pairs. *Falsified if* fewer than 4 of 6 pairs reach the 95th percentile on CAGR, or fewer than 4 of 6 have a shallower drawdown than the N-A median.
  - **H2 (leg choice):** given the rule's own timing, holding the cheap leg beats holding a randomly chosen leg (null N-B) at the 95th percentile on after-cost CAGR in at least 4 of the 6 index pairs. *Falsified if* fewer than 4 of 6.
- **Null table** (Appendix B format) — **N-A decides**:

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in 4.0 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| *when the sleeve is in a leg* (the ingredient of H1) | randomised (N-A) | the real decision sequence is cut into blocks (maximal runs of abutting trade spells, e.g. A for 5 weeks straight into B for 3) and the neutral gaps between them; each draw permutes the blocks among the block positions and the non-zero gaps among the non-zero-gap positions (a zero gap can only sit at either end and stays there), then rebuilds the target sequence over the same decision weeks; 2,000 draws; seed `default_rng([pair_id, exit_id, draw_id, window])` | — |
| *which leg is held* (the ingredient of H2) | randomised (N-B) | the real timing kept; each block's legs flipped together by one fair coin (A ↔ B), which keeps the alternation inside a block; 2,000 draws; seed `default_rng([100 + pair_id, exit_id, draw_id, window])` | — |
| pair, calendar, decision weeks, fill rule, cost | held | constant | — |
| number of trade spells, each spell's length, number of switches, weeks neutral; weeks in leg a / leg b (N-A) or in a trade (N-B) | held | by construction of the permutation / flip | counts, strategy vs every draw: must be identical |
| the legs' own trend (a draw may sit in a leg during a different market regime) | not matched | — | volatility and drawdown of the draws vs the strategy, shown |
| the neutral book's drift (50/50 bought at the time of entering neutral, then unrebalanced) | not matched | — | shown as the 50/50 comparators |

- **Statistic, direction, why:** as T01 — after-cost CAGR of the daily-valued sleeve, higher is better (the return the sleeve earns); max drawdown of the daily NAV, shallower is better (the risk the sleeve carries). Both per pair.
- **Pass mark and number of draws:** 95th percentile of 2,000 draws for CAGR (p ≤ 0.05); drawdown shallower than the null median. Percentile = (draws worse + 0.5 × ties) ÷ draws × 100; p = (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1); edge = strategy − null median.
- **Validity gates:**
  - **V1** the independent verifier (`verify_ratio.py --ratio plain`; no engine import; the form comes from this design through run.sh, not from the engine) re-derives every decision on P_a ÷ P_b and reproduces every pair-setting's final NAV, CAGR and max drawdown within 1e-9; it fails if audit.json names any form other than plain; and it fails on a deliberately corrupted copy (a flipped decision; a scaled NAV).
  - **V2** no look-ahead: the engine asserts for every decision that the band uses only decision weeks ≤ t and that the trade date is strictly after the decision date; check count > 0, violations = 0.
  - **V3** the null held what it promised: for every draw, the counts in the null table equal the strategy's.
  - **V4** regression: (a) the "always neutral" sleeve (50/50 bought at the first trade, never traded again) equals the closed-form NAV from the two legs' prices within 1e-9; (b) the engine as changed for T02, run with T01's form (`--test T01`, 0 draws), reproduces every row of T01's `evidence/headline/summary.csv` — final NAV, CAGR and max drawdown — within 1e-9, so the change to the shared engine did not move T01.
- **Reportability gate:** a pair-setting with fewer than 4 round trips or fewer than 200 decision weeks is **NOT REPORTABLE**: no number from it is quoted, and it counts as *not passing* in the 4-of-6 counts.
- **Pass criteria:**
  - **C1** after-cost CAGR ≥ the 95th percentile of N-A (2,000 draws) in ≥ 4 of the 6 index pairs, exit E1, window 52, cost 20.8 bps.
  - **C2** max drawdown shallower (less negative) than the N-A median in ≥ 4 of 6, same setting.
  - **C3** after-cost CAGR ≥ the 95th percentile of N-B in ≥ 4 of 6, same setting.
  - H1 passes iff C1 and C2; H2 passes iff C3.
- **Robustness checks:**
  - **R1** costs 0 / 20.8 / 50 bps round trip (the decisions do not change; the numbers and the null do).
  - **R2** band window 26 and 104 weeks (52 is the reference, the middle of the grid).
  - **R3** exit E2 — hold until the ratio reaches the opposite band (or 26 weeks) — through the same criteria; **E1 is the deciding exit**, E2 is reported.
  - **R4** sub-periods, descriptive only (no null): 2006–2015, 2016–2019, 2020–2023, 2024–2026 — CAGR and drawdown of the sleeve vs 50/50.
  - **R5** the 4 gold pairs through the same code, reported, never counted.
- **Multiplicity:** within T02, as T01: 2 exits × 3 windows = 6 decision settings per pair, 3 cost levels, 10 pairs; the reference setting is fixed; a claim about the best-looking non-reference setting is judged at the 98th percentile of its own null, with the number of settings beside it. Across tests: the ratio form is the second form tried on the same pairs and window, chosen after T01's result was known. C1–C3 keep T01's 95th percentile so that the two tests read side by side; a T02 pass is therefore PROVISIONAL at most, and a statement that "the plain form works" additionally needs C1 at the 97.5th percentile (0.05 ÷ 2 forms) in ≥ 4 of 6, printed by analyse.py beside C1.
- **Reference setting:** exit E1, window 52 weeks, cost 20.8 bps, max hold 26 weeks — T01's, chosen by position, never by result.
- **The MDE:** 2.8 × the standard deviation of the N-A CAGR distribution for that pair (80% power, 5% two-sided), computed by analyse.py and printed beside every FAIL as "undetectable below X pp a year".
- **Descriptive only (no claims):** the per-trade "extra" (the held leg's fill-to-fill return minus the peer's over the hold); the share of trades positive; the band charts; the gold pairs; the sub-periods; the touches not traded because the sleeve was already in a trade; and **T02 against T01** — per pair at the reference setting, the decision weeks whose state differs between the two forms, the round trips under each, the entries on the same week with the same target, and the CAGR and N-A percentile under each (read from tests/T01/evidence/headline/ and this test's evidence).

## 3. Methodology                     (BEFORE)

- **Data table:**

| file | contributes | through-date | review verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| data/index_ohlcv_all.csv (nifty100, midcap100, smallcap100, microcap250) | daily OHLC: the signal (close), the fill (OHLC4), the valuation (close) | 2026-08-28 | PASS WITH NOTES |
| data/MAUc1_daily.csv | daily OHLC of the gold leg (gold pairs only) | 2026-09-08 | PASS WITH NOTES |

- **Construction, step by step** (T01 §3 with step 4 changed; steps 9–10 written as T01 ran):
  1. Each leg's daily bars come through `analysis/approved_data.load_daily()` (fingerprint checked; the loader rules H1–H3 of the data review applied; blank closes dropped).
  2. A pair's **calendar** is the intersection of its two legs' trading days. Nothing is carried forward.
  3. **Decision days** are the last calendar day of each ISO week (a Friday unless Friday is a holiday for either leg). The **trade day** for a decision is the next calendar day.
  4. The **ratio** is r_t = P_a,t ÷ P_b,t on decision-day closes, **not logged**, where a is the pair's first asset in the order N100, MC100, SC100, Micro250, GoldMCX — T01's orientation (N100 ÷ MC100, …, Micro250 ÷ GoldMCX). The **band** at decision week t is the mean and standard deviation (ddof 1) of r over the trailing W = 52 decision weeks ending at and including t, requiring at least 42 of them; before that there is no band and no decision. This is the only change from T01, whose series was ln(P_a,t ÷ P_b,t).
  5. **State** is one of neutral / A (100% in leg a) / B (100% in leg b). From neutral: if r_t < mean_t − sd_t and the week before had a band and r_{t−1} was not below its band (the first week of a run beyond the lower band) → A; if r_t > mean_t + sd_t and the week before had a band and was not above it → B. A touch while already in a trade is ignored (counted). **Exit E1:** in A, exit when r_t ≥ mean_t; in B, when r_t ≤ mean_t; or after 26 decision weeks in the trade. **Exit E2:** in A, exit when r_t > mean_t + sd_t; in B, when r_t < mean_t − sd_t; or after 26 weeks. An exit and an entry on the same decision week are both taken (the sleeve passes straight from A to B when the ratio jumps across the band).
  6. **The book:** NAV starts at 1.0. On the first decision week that has a band the sleeve buys 50/50 by value on the trade day; the first valuation is the calendar day *before* that trade day, so the first trade's cost is inside the measured path. On a switch the sleeve sells what it must and buys the target on the trade day at each leg's `fill` price (OHLC4, or the close on a stale-OHL row); the cost is 10.4 bps of the value traded on each side. Neutral = 50/50 by value at the moment it is entered, then unrebalanced until the next switch. Units are held; NAV every calendar day = Σ units × close. No cash, no financing, no slippage beyond the flat cost.
  7. **Statistics** per pair-setting-series: CAGR = NAV_end^(365.25 ÷ days) − 1 over the valuation window; max drawdown = min over days of NAV ÷ running max − 1; annualised vol = sd of daily log returns × √252; final NAV; round trips; weeks in A / B / neutral; switches.
  8. **Comparators:** hold a; hold b; 50/50 bought at the first trade and never rebalanced; 50/50 rebalanced every decision week at the fill, with cost (constant mix); "always neutral" = the sleeve with no trades (equals comparator 3 — gate V4a). None of them depends on the ratio form, so they equal T01's.
  9. **Null N-A:** the strategy's decision sequence as blocks and gaps (null table) → permuted → the same book code (step 6). **Null N-B:** the same blocks, each block's legs flipped by one coin → the same book code. Both run at every window and exit at the reference cost, and at every cost on the reference window.
  10. Everything is written to `evidence/<run>/`: `summary.csv` (one row per pair × window × exit × cost × series), `draws_<pair>_<W>_<exit>_<cost>.csv` (every draw's CAGR, drawdown, counts, counts_ok), `decisions.csv` (one row per decision week: date, `ratio` = r_t, mean, sd, position, state before, state after, reason), `trades.csv` (one row per trade: decision date, trade date, from, to, fills, value traded, cost), `trade_extras.csv` (the per-trade extra), `nav_<pair>_<W>_<exit>_<cost>.csv` (daily; regenerated by run.sh, not kept in git), `audit.json` (test = T02, ratio = plain and its formula, fingerprints, rules, seeds, PIT check count and violations, null counts, stale-row count), `verify.log`, and `t01_regression/summary.csv` for gate V4b.
- **How the test works, in plain words:** take N100 ÷ SC100, the plain ratio of the two index levels. Every Friday close we compare it with its last-52-week average and spread. The first Friday it is more than one spread below the average, N100 has lagged SC100 unusually: on Monday the sleeve sells its SC100 and holds only N100. It keeps N100 until the first Friday the ratio is back at its average (E1), or 26 weeks, then goes back to 50/50 on the Monday. Each trade costs 10.4 bps of what is traded, and the sleeve is marked every day. To see whether *when* mattered, the same trades (same legs, same lengths, abutting trades kept together) are scattered at random over the same weeks 2,000 times; to see whether *which leg* mattered, the real timing is kept and a coin picks the leg, 2,000 times. Everything is T01 except that the average and the spread are of the ratio itself, not of its logarithm.
- **Point-in-time rule:** the band at decision week t is computed from the weekly series truncated at t (the engine passes `.iloc[:i+1]` and asserts its last index equals the decision date); the fill is taken on the next calendar day and the engine asserts `trade_date > decision_date`; every assertion is counted in audit.json (gate V2).
- **Execution and cost:** decide at the decision day's close; trade the next calendar day at `fill` (OHLC4); 10.4 bps per side on value traded; no financing; no slippage model beyond that; no tax.
- **Effective sample size and MDE:** the independent unit is a round trip; under the log form T01 had 34–41 round trips on the three 20-year index pairs and 6–9 on the three Micro250 pairs at the reference setting, and T02 is expected to be close to that, since the two forms mostly agree; the MDE (2.8 × sd of the N-A CAGR) is reported next to every FAIL and is expected to be of the order of T01's 2.6–11.4 pp a year.
- **Known contaminants:**

| issue | handling |
|---|---|
| price indices, no dividends | both legs alike; affects levels, not the relative test; stated |
| gold front-month continuation (roll inside the return; 2026-01-30 open item) | gold pairs reported, never counted |
| Micro250 from 2022 only | its three pairs are reportable only if ≥ 4 round trips and ≥ 200 decision weeks; the short window is stated beside them |
| orientation (plain form only) | fixed as T01's before the run (step 4); the reverse orientation is not run and will be listed in §6 as not tested |
| the band's units (plain form only) | the sd is in ratio units, not percentage units, so where the ratio's level moves a lot inside one window the band is lopsided relative to the log form; the trailing window re-centres every week; this is the difference under test, not a defect |
| the second form tried on the same window, after seeing the first | C1 at the 97.5th printed beside C1; any pass is PROVISIONAL; a hold-out is the real test |
| the 2008 crash inside the index-pair window | a sub-period in R4 |
| no hold-out | as T01; a hold-out on data after 2026-09 is the first next step if anything passes |
